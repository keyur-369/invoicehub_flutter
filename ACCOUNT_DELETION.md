# InvoiceHub Account & Data Deletion Request

**Developer:** UniBuild Apps  
**Application:** InvoiceHub  
**Contact Email:** support@unibuildapps.com  

---

## How Users Can Request Account & Data Deletion

Users of **InvoiceHub** have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated data at any time.

### Option 1: Request via Email
Send an email to **support@unibuildapps.com** from your registered account email with:
- **Subject:** `InvoiceHub Account Deletion Request`
- **Details:** State your registered email address and request account deletion.

### Option 2: Request In-App
1. Open **InvoiceHub**.
2. Go to **Settings** > **Account Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete Account & Data**.

---

## Types of Data Deleted
Upon account deletion, the following data is permanently purged:
- User Authentication Credentials (Email, User ID)
- Business Profile & Logo
- Customer Records & Contacts
- Invoices, Items, Receipts, and Billing History

---

## Data Processing & Retention Period
- Deletion requests are processed within **7 business days**.
- All database backups are completely purged within **30 days**.
